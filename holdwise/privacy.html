HoldWise: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: MP Tech DE, Berlin, Germany
Contact: marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com
App: HoldWise: Video Poker Trainer for Android (package de.mptech.holdwise)

The short version. HoldWise is a video poker trainer for adults. It uses virtual credits only: there is no real-money gambling, credits cannot be bought and they have no cash value. The app has no accounts and no chat, and your progress stays on your device. We do not sell your data. The app uses Google services for ads, the optional Remove Ads purchase, usage statistics, crash reports and remote settings, as explained below. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you choose what you allow in a consent form, and you can change that choice in the app at any time.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your virtual credits, statistics, coach rank, daily challenge progress and settings in its private storage. We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your personal Google account backup, under Google's terms. We have no access to that backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

HoldWise shows a banner below the table, an occasional full-screen ad between hands (never during a decision), and optional reward videos that you choose to watch for extra virtual credits. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions. Legal basis in the EEA and UK: your consent where required (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Ads are never for real-money gambling.

Usage statistics, crash reports and remote settings (Google Firebase)

If you allow it, the app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the app is used and to fix errors, and Firebase Remote Config to adjust settings such as how often ads may appear. Events describe app use only, such as a hand completed, a trainer answer, the daily challenge completed, an ad shown or a purchase attempted. Firebase may process app instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and crash logs. We do not use this data for advertising. Where consent is required and you refuse it, this collection stays off.

Your consent choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives Google Play's confirmation of the purchase. Virtual credits are never sold.

Notifications

Practice reminders are off by default. If you turn them on, the app schedules them on your device only. No server is involved.

Internet access

The game works offline. Internet access is used to load ads, check purchases and, if allowed, send statistics and crash reports and receive remote settings.

Sharing and retention

Data processed through the services above is handled by Google as described in its Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google may process data outside your country under its data transfer safeguards. We do not sell personal data. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Firebase, at most 14 months.

Age

HoldWise contains simulated gambling and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it. For data processed by Google, use your Google Account tools. For any request, write to marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app changes how it handles data. The effective date above shows the latest version.
