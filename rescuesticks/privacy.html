Rescue Sticks: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: MP Tech DE, Berlin, Germany
Contact: marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com
App: Rescue Sticks: Cat & Dog Game for Android (package de.mptech.rescuesticks)

The short version. Rescue Sticks is a puzzle game for all ages. It has no accounts, no chat and no user-generated content. Your progress stays on your device. When the game starts, a neutral age screen asks for the year of birth. Players under 13, and players who skip the question, see no ads at all, and no advertising or analytics data is collected from them. For players aged 13 and over, Google services are used for ads, optional purchases, usage statistics and crash reports, as explained below. We do not sell personal data.

All animal rescues happen inside the game. Rescue Sticks does not collect donations and is not affiliated with any animal welfare organization.

Age screen

The year of birth is stored only on your device and is used only to choose suitable settings: whether ads may be shown, whether they may be personalised, and whether statistics may be collected. It is never sent to us or to advertising partners.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps your progress (levels, stars, rescued animals, treats, tools, daily streak, classic mode scores and settings) in the app's private storage. We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your personal Google account backup, under Google's terms.

Advertising (players aged 13 and over only)

For players aged 13 and over, Rescue Sticks may show a banner on menu screens (adults only), an occasional full-screen ad at a calm pause after a completed rescue, and optional reward videos you choose to watch for an extra heart or a hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) and, for adults, partner networks through AdMob mediation (Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize and Equativ). To deliver, measure and protect ads, these services may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interactions.

  • Under 13 or age not given: the ads SDK is not started; no ad requests are made and no advertising ID is used.
  • 13 to 17: only non-personalised ads, with content suitable for a general audience.
  • 18 and over: personalised ads only where you consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US states); otherwise non-personalised ads.

Legal basis in the EEA and UK: your consent where required (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads.

Usage statistics and crash reports (players aged 13 and over, with consent)

If you are 13 or over and allow it, the app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to understand how the game is played and to fix errors. Events describe gameplay only, such as a level started or completed, a hint used or a purchase attempted. Advertising ID collection by Analytics is turned off. Firebase may process app instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and crash logs. We do not use this data for advertising. For players under 13 or of unknown age, this collection is always off.

Your consent choices

Where required, the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Purchases

Optional purchases (Remove ads, Starter pack, rescue tool packs) are handled entirely by Google Play, in real money and at the price shown by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the app only receives Google Play's confirmation. Purchases by children are subject to Google Play's parental controls.

Reminders

Optional reminders are off by default and are scheduled on your device only. No server is involved.

Internet access

The game works offline. Internet access is used to load ads (13+), check purchases and, if allowed, send statistics and crash reports.

Sharing and retention

Data processed through the services above is handled by Google and the named ad partners as described in their privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Data may be processed outside your country under appropriate transfer safeguards. We do not sell personal data. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Firebase, at most 14 months.

Children

Rescue Sticks is suitable for all ages and is offered to a mixed audience in line with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: for them the game shows no ads and sends no analytics or crash data. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to a data protection authority. You can delete everything the game stores by uninstalling it. For data processed by Google, use your Google Account tools. For any request, write to marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com.

How to delete your data (Rescue Sticks by MP Tech DE)

Rescue Sticks has no accounts, so there is nothing to close. To delete your data:

  1. Game progress on your device: uninstall Rescue Sticks, or open Android Settings > Apps > Rescue Sticks > Storage > Clear data. Progress, settings, the stored year of birth and reminders are deleted at once and cannot be restored.
  2. Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase, players 13+ who allowed them): email marcelpinto.berlin@gmail.com with the subject "Rescue Sticks data deletion" and we will delete any data we can match to your request within 30 days. Because the game has no accounts, these statistics are not linked to your name or email, so we may not be able to find individual records; in every case they are deleted automatically when the Firebase retention period (at most 14 months) ends. Clearing the app's data (step 1) also resets the app instance identifier, so new statistics are not linked to old ones.
  3. Advertising data (players 13+): we do not hold it. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, and use your Google Account tools for data held by Google.
  4. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required for payments and taxes; we do not store them.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app changes how it handles data. The effective date above shows the latest version.
